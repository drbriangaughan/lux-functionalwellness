Standard container for services, process steps and partner tracks.

```jsx
<Card interactive title="Functional bloodwork review">
  <p>Read through a functional lens — not just whether you are "in range", but whether you are optimal.</p>
</Card>
<Card variant="inverse" topRule={false} eyebrow="For chiropractors" title="Co-management partnership">…</Card>
```

The 4px gold top rule is the brand's card signature — keep it on marketing cards, turn it off (`topRule={false}`)
for plain in-product panels. Hover lifts 4px. Never a colored *left* border.
