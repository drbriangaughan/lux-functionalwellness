Text entry for intake forms, booking and the patient portal.

```jsx
<Input label="Email" type="email" hint="We reply within one business day." />
<Input label="Phone" error="Enter a 10-digit number." />
<Textarea label="What are you hoping to resolve?" rows={5} />
```

Labels are sentence case and 13px semibold; hints sit below in subtle sand. Focus = royal-blue border plus the gold focus ring.
