The standard action control. Gold `accent` is the default and carries nearly every CTA; `onDark` is its
partner on navy bands.

```jsx
<Button size="lg">Book your review</Button>
<Button variant="onDark" size="lg">Ask us a question first</Button>
<Button variant="secondary">Talk to us about partnering</Button>
```

Square corners, uppercase label, 0.12em tracking. Hover lifts 2px and lightens gold to `--gold-300`;
nothing scales or shrinks. Labels are verb + object, two to four words.
