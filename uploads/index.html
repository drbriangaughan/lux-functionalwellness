Our Philosophy

Health.

True wellness isn't complicated — it's consistent. At LUX, we believe that small, sustainable changes in nutrition, lifestyle, and supplementation build the foundation for lasting change.

Your blood doesn't lie. It tells the story of what your body needs right now, and we use that story to help you build habits that fit your real life — not an idealized version of it.

Clarity Through Data

Functional bloodwork panels reveal what standard labs miss — giving you a precise, personalized starting point rather than guesswork.

Habits Over Hacks

We don't sell shortcuts. We work with you to build realistic, meaningful changes you can sustain — day by day, year by year.

Whole-Person Care

Nutrition, supplementation, and lifestyle don't exist in isolation. We look at the full picture so that every recommendation connects to how you actually feel and function.

Collaborative by Design

We work alongside your existing care team — whether that's your chiropractor, your trainer, or your primary care physician — not instead of them.

The Process

How It Works

Simple, virtual, and built around your schedule.

01

Book Your Consultation

Schedule a brief intake call so we can understand your health history, goals, and what's not working right now.

02

Order Your Labs

We order a comprehensive functional bloodwork panel through our lab partner. You get drawn at a location near you — no hospital required.

03

Virtual Review

We meet virtually to walk through your results in plain language — what's optimal, what needs attention, and why it matters for how you feel.

04

Your Wellness Plan

You receive a personalized protocol covering diet, supplementation, and lifestyle changes — specific to your results, built for your real life.

05

Ongoing Support

Health is a journey, not a one-time visit. We schedule follow-ups and retest to track your progress and adjust as you improve.
